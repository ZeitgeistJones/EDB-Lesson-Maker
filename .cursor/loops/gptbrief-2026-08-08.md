## Pack 2 — feelings emotion faces, regen with distinct cues  (MEDIUM, has a caveat)

Why ChatGPT: 6–7 emotion faces that sit in ONE feelings dock and must be mutually distinguishable and share a style. Open row in `content-wishlist.md` (2026-08-08): worried/scared/confused currently look too similar; judges want VISUAL cues (tears=scared, sweat=worried, furrowed brow=confused) — NOT word labels (S58 forbids answer-naming).

Caveat / risk: the value here is subtle per-face cue accuracy. A contact sheet may come back with cues too faint or wrong. If the returned sheet is ambiguous, DO NOT ship it — fall back to `.cursor/skills/prop-cutouts` in-house **one-at-a-time** so each cue is controlled. Send this only if you want a fast first draft.

```
CHATGPT PROMPT — feeling-faces (3x3 = 7 props, use 7 of 9 cells, leave 2 empty)
Draw 7 separate round cartoon "feeling" faces for an ESL emotions lesson,
arranged in a clean 3 rows × 3 columns grid (fill 7 cells, leave the last 2 empty and black).
Requirements:
- Pure solid BLACK background/field. No grey grid, no labels, NO WORDS, no cell titles.
- ONE face per cell, its own margin, nothing crossing a cell border.
- Flat, bright, kid-friendly vector style, same head shape and line weight across all 7 (one coherent set).
- Each face MUST be unmistakably different by a VISUAL cue, no text:
  1. happy — big open smile, bright eyes
  2. shy — small smile, blush cheeks, eyes glancing aside
  3. confused — furrowed brow, one raised eyebrow, slightly open mouth
  4. worried — small frown + a sweat drop on the temple
  5. scared — wide eyes + tear drops, mouth open
  6. surprised — round wide eyes + round open "o" mouth, raised brows
  7. proud — chin up, closed confident smile, sparkle
Export as one PNG.
```

WHEN THE SHEET COMES BACK:
```
save to assets-inbox/feeling-faces.png, then:
```
