# Copy-paste prompts for new board art

For generating in the ChatGPT app (free) rather than the paid API. Choose the
**landscape** aspect ratio, save the result as PNG, and drop it into
`assets-inbox/` — then tell the agent and it will crop, resize and register it.

**Prefer quiet themed flat sets for most lessons** — see
[`bg-theme-sets.md`](bg-theme-sets.md). Place scenes below are only when the
**scene itself** is the hero (empty centre band for standing props).

Four a day covers a batch of scenes in a sitting. The first twenty are already
wired (hotel through lake-dock). Use this file when adding more.

## The style anchor

Every scene prompt below already includes this. It exists because the 40 scenes
already in the bank share one look, and a new one that does not match is worse
than no new one at all.

> Flat vector illustration, soft muted pastel palette, no harsh outlines, gentle
> ambient shading, subtle depth. Eye-level camera, wide symmetrical view. The
> central 60% of the image must be empty open floor or ground — all furniture
> and scenery sits along the far left and far right edges only. The wall meets
> the floor roughly 40% of the way down the image. No people, no animals, no
> text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no signage lettering. Calm,
> uncluttered, cheerful. Composed as a wide panoramic banner.

## Word pictures (only if the free scenes go well)

These are the words with no honest picture. They are little scenes rather than
objects, which is why no free icon set has them. Ask for all four of a pair in
**one** image and the importer can slice them, or generate singly.

- **cramped / spacious** — the same small room drawn twice: once crowded wall to
  wall with furniture, once nearly empty with one chair
- **tidy / cluttered** — the same child's bedroom drawn twice: once with
  everything put away, once with toys and clothes over the floor
- **cosy** — an armchair with a blanket and a warm lamp in a corner
- **tray / queue / napkin / delicious** — school cafeteria objects and a line of
  three plain figures seen from behind
- **security / turbulence / connection** — airport security arch, an aeroplane
  in bumpy air with a wavy motion trail, a departure board showing two linked
  flights
- **swing** — a playground swing seat on chains, empty, side view
- **doctor / nurse pair** — two clearly different characters, one in a white
  coat with a stethoscope, one in coloured scrubs

Style for these: same flat vector look, centred single subject, plain white or
fully transparent background, no text.

## Rules that still apply

- Nothing scraped. If a prompt will not produce something honest, the word goes
  to [`asset-wishlist.md`](asset-wishlist.md) and the lesson uses a plain card.
- Anything generated goes in the ledger at `public/assets/LICENSE.md`.
- No lettering inside art. ClassIn boards put the real text on top, and baked-in
  words cannot be corrected or translated.
