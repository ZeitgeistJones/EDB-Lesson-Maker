# Scene sheet 02 — place backgrounds

Second batch of scene gaps. Same rules as `scenes-01-places.md`: one image at
a time at 1280×590, empty centre band, all detail in the outer thirds, and a
measured `groundY` added to
`public/assets/08_backgrounds/manifest.json`.

| Slug | Category | Tags |
|---|---|---|
| `gas-station` | commercial | gas, petrol, car, fuel |
| `snowy-street` | outdoor | snow, winter, cold, street |
| `party-room` | home | party, birthday, celebrate, cake |
| `museum` | commercial | museum, art, visit, trip |
| `laundromat` | commercial | laundry, wash, clothes, chores |
| `stadium` | sports | stadium, sports, game, crowd |

Use the shared prompt frame from `scenes-01-places.md`, with these scene lines.

## Scene lines

1) **gas-station** — a small gas station forecourt: flat paved forecourt running across the whole width, one fuel pump and a bin on the left third, a small shop front with a blank window and an air pump on the right third, plain sky behind, empty centre. No brand marks, no price signs, no numbers on the pump.

2) **snowy-street** — a quiet snowy town street: snow-covered pavement running across the whole width, a lamp post and a bare tree on the left third, a shop front with a blank awning and a snow-topped bench on the right third, pale winter sky above low buildings, empty centre. Light falling snow is fine, keep it faint.

3) **party-room** — a living room set up for a birthday: wooden floor across the whole width, a table with a plain undecorated cake and cups on the left third, a chair and a stack of wrapped presents on the right third, bunting and balloons high up along the top edge only, plain wall behind, empty centre. No writing on the bunting, no candles spelling anything.

4) **museum** — a museum gallery: polished floor across the whole width, a display plinth with a simple vase and a rope barrier on the left third, a bench and a second plinth on the right third, plain gallery wall behind with empty picture frames hung high, empty centre. Frames must be blank — no paintings, no labels.

5) **laundromat** — a laundromat interior: tiled floor across the whole width, a row of front-loading washing machines along the left third, a folding counter with a basket and a chair on the right third, plain wall behind, empty centre. No control-panel numbers, no signs.

6) **stadium** — a sports stadium seen from pitch level: green pitch with a white line running across the whole width, a dugout bench and a corner flag on the left third, a goal post edge and a scoreboard stand on the right third, blocks of empty coloured seating in the background, empty centre pitch. Scoreboard must be blank — no numbers.
