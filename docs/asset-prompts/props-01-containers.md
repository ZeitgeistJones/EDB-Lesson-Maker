# Prop sheet 01 — containers (drag-into targets)

Six open containers a student can drag vocabulary pieces *into*. The bank has
a shelf, a bin and a tray; every one of these is a different silhouette and a
different put-away story (pack the bag, post the letter, load the tray).

Slugs, panel order:
`toy-box` `backpack-open` `suitcase` `shopping-basket` `lunch-tray` `mailbox`

Roles: `container` for all six (`sortBin` for the basket and tray if a recipe
wants a second sort target).

---

Generate a 2×3 grid of classroom prop cutouts for an interactive English lesson whiteboard (2 rows, 3 columns). Overall size 3840×1180 so each panel is exactly 1280×590. Panels divided evenly, NO white gutters or divider lines.

Every panel: solid pure black background. Flat vector, clean and simple, soft muted colors, consistent style and lighting across all six. Mostly front-on, very slight 3/4 tilt allowed where the inside should be visible. ONE object per panel, fully visible, centered, ~5% margin from edges. No people, animals, text, letters, numbers, logos, labels, or writing of any kind.

These are drop targets — a student drags picture cards INTO them. All EMPTY / open so there is room to place things.

6 props, left→right, top→bottom:

1) Open toy box — light wood or muted pastel crate, lid hinged open and leaning back, inside completely empty and visible.
2) Open backpack — muted blue or green school backpack, front-on, main flap open showing an empty interior, straps visible, no brand marks, no patches.
3) Open suitcase — small hard-shell travel case, muted tan or slate, lid open flat like a clamshell, both halves empty, simple handle.
4) Shopping basket — hand basket, muted red or grey plastic with a folded handle, seen slightly from above, completely empty.
5) School lunch tray — muted cream or pale green moulded tray with 4 empty compartments, seen slightly from above so the wells read clearly, nothing on it.
6) Mailbox — simple standing letterbox, muted blue or dark red, small door open showing an empty slot, flag up, NO letters, NO numbers, NO writing on it.

Black backgrounds only. Consistent lighting. Premium cutouts ready to crop.
