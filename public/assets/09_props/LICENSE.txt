BOARD PROPS (cutouts)
=====================

PNG props with real alpha channels, for ClassIn collage / activity pieces.
Each one was generated on a solid black field — the image models have no
transparency — and then keyed to a cutout by scripts/import-prop.mjs. The
raw black-field originals are banked in assets-inbox/raw/.

Most of the pack was generated with OpenAI image models for this project;
ownership of that output sits with the account holder under OpenAI's terms.
The newer props (backpack, suitcase, swing, umbrella, reward-jar-v2) were
generated with Cursor's native image model for this project.
Verify current terms before commercial resale.

Sets:
  - Covers (green/blue/orange/purple) + prize flap + green bin
  - Dress-up accessories (hat, glasses, scarf, bag, bow, cap)
  - Speaking / feedback (mic, check, arrow, target, gift, hourglass)
  - Furniture shells (table, chair, door, window, rug, lamp)
  - Phonics tools (tiles, sound boxes, word strip, pencil, eraser)
  - Containers and objects (backpack, suitcase, umbrella, jar + lid, swing)
  - Earlier shells (shelf, bin, flashcard, sticky, jar, tray, speech, star, slot)

Six props are still black-backed rather than keyed: sorting-bin-green,
prize-flap, tile-blue, sound-boxes, word-strip and pencil. They were cut from
contact sheets or carry a colour wash to the frame border, so nothing above the
black threshold can be separated cleanly. See docs/asset-wishlist.md.

See manifest.json for roles, tags, aspect, relativeScale, anchor and bodyHue.
