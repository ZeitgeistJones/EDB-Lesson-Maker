BOARD PROPS (cutouts)
=====================

PNG props on black backgrounds for ClassIn collage / activity pieces.
Generated with OpenAI image models for this project.
Ownership of output sits with the account holder under OpenAI's terms.
Verify current terms before commercial resale.

Sets:
  - Covers (green/blue/orange/purple) + prize flap + green bin
  - Dress-up accessories (hat, glasses, scarf, bag, bow, cap)
  - Speaking / feedback (mic, check, arrow, target, gift, hourglass)
  - Furniture shells (table, chair, door, window, rug, lamp)
  - Phonics tools (tiles, sound boxes, word strip, pencil, eraser)
  - Earlier shells (shelf, bin, flashcard, sticky, jar, tray, speech, star, slot)

See manifest.json for roles/tags.
