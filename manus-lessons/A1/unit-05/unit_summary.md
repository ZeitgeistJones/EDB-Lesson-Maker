# Unit 05 — School Day

**CEFR:** A1  
**Communicative goal:** Learners name school things and say what they have or need.  
**World:** Maple Street School, featuring Mia, Leo, and Zip the school-bag mascot.

| Lesson | Topic | Core language | Final learner product | Self-rating |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | What’s in my bag? | `What’s this? — It’s a ___.` | A five-item show-and-tell bag | 9.1/10 |
| 2 | I have / I need | `I have a ___. / I need a ___.` | A personal have/need bag plan | 9.1/10 |
| 3 | Classroom treasure hunt | `Where is the ___? — It’s here.` | A three-stop classroom treasure map | 9.2/10 |
| 4 | Share please | `Can I have a ___, please? — Here you are. Thank you.` | A two-item helpful-table roleplay | 9.2/10 |
| 5 | Ready for school | `I have a ___. I’m ready!` | A personal ready-bag showcase | 9.3/10 |

## Unit Continuity

The five lessons intentionally build from **naming familiar school things** to distinguishing **have / need**, locating classroom objects, completing a polite sharing exchange, and finally integrating those skills in a meaningful **ready-for-school** task. Recycled language reappears in new learner jobs rather than through duplicated board sequences.
