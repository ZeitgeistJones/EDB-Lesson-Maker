# Unit 04 — Pet Pals: Production Summary

## Delivered Lesson Arc

| Lesson | Topic | Core output | Distinct activity identity |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the pets | `It’s a ___` | Discovery walk, clue windows, pet-camera hunt, home matching, passport |
| 2 | I have a pet | `I have / I don’t have a ___` | Ownership models, fictional-profile sorting, café roleplay, mini card |
| 3 | Pet care | `I feed my ___ / Let’s walk the dog` | Action lab, care route, feeding, water gauges, care-clock plan |
| 4 | Lost pet mystery | `Where is my ___? / Look! It’s here!` | Detective clues, kitchen search, carrot trail, reveal, retell, roleplay |
| 5 | Pet show | `This is my ___ / I like my ___` | Stage rehearsal, spotlight listening, show badge, parade performance |

## Final Verification

The completed package contains five related but non-repetitive A1 lessons. Each lesson contains exactly ten 1920 × 1080 PNG boards and one multipage PDF. The unit’s learner independence increases from recognition and supported naming in Lesson 1 to a visible, two-sentence pet-show presentation in Lesson 5. The production system reuses stable art direction and quality gates without reusing a mechanical board sequence.

> The board images are polished static visual boards. All movement, snapping, revealing, and persistence stated in the implementation handoffs must be configured in the native lesson-board editor.
