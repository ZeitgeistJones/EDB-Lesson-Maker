# Lesson 05 — Meet the Class

**CEFR:** A1  
**Topic:** Meet the class  
**Communicative objective:** Integrate greeting + name + goodbye in a class party mission.  
**Target vocabulary:** hello, name, friend, goodbye  
**Target language:** Hello, I’m ___. What’s your name? Nice to meet you. Goodbye!

> **Implementation note:** Each PNG is a finished visual board. Native dragging, snapping, click reveals, editable text entry, and state persistence must be configured in the teaching board editor; static images do not claim to provide those functions.
